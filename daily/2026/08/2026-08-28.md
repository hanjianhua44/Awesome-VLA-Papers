In this paper, we introduce Composed Gait Retrieval (CoGR), a novel task that retrieves a target gait sequence based on a reference sequence and a natural language modification query. To address the absence of existing datasets for this task, we design an automated annotation pipeline powered by large vision-language models (VLMs) to construct the first gait-language datasets: Language-Augmented CCPG and Language-Augmented CASIA-B

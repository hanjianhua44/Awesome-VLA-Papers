### 🔥 #6｜Think Only When Needed: Prompt-Authority Control for Selective Slow-Path Intervention in Vision-Language-Action Manipulation

📎 [2608.23224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23224) | `cs.RO cs.AI cs.CV`

### 🔥 #1｜AdaRSI: On-Demand Recursive Self-Improvement for Embodied Agents

**Peidong Wang, Ying Chang, Zhiming Ma 等 (11 位作者)** |  `CAS, Northeastern Univ`

📎 [2610.20474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20474) | `cs.CL cs.RO`

> Pretrained vision-language-action models (VLAs) often fail midway through unseen long-horizon tasks. Recursive self-improvement (RSI) offers a remedy, but embodied RSI methods either refine the harness around a frozen executor and so cannot add a missing motion, or train the executor on persistent failures without asking whether a failure lies in how the executor is called or in what it can do. We ask when a self-improving embodied system should change the weights of its executor, and propose AdaRSI, an on-demand RSI framework for embodied agents

### 🔥 #2｜Graph-Reweighted Behavior Cloning with Human Interventions for Precise Robotic Manipulation

**Xiaoman Li, Zhiqiang Yang, Jiayu Hu 等 (9 位作者)** |  `Tsinghua, ZJU, USTC, SCUT`

📎 [2610.20504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20504) | `cs.RO`

> The key challenge is to identify which action chunks actually advance the task, rather than uniformly cloning or discarding imperfect trajectories. Reverse graph search computes minimal remaining steps to task success within the dataset support, and each action chunk is weighted by how much its execution reduces this potential. This consequence-based weighting reuses recoverable segments from failed trajectories, leverages interventions as recovery paths, and down-weights locally regressive chunks even inside successful trajectories. New rollouts are incorporated by appending them to the graph and rerunning search, without training a reward model, action-conditioned critic, or progress predictor
>

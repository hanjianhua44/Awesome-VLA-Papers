### 🔥 #1｜EmbodiRSI: Recursive Self-Improvement for Data-Efficient Robot Adaptation

**Haoran Lang, Haotao Lu, Shiyu Sang 等 (9 位作者)** |  `ShanghaiTech`

📎 [2609.38905](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38905) | `cs.RO`

> Adapting robot manipulation policies to new tasks and environments remains highly data-intensive, while the data needed for further improvement depends on the policy's current capabilities and failure modes. We introduce EmbodiRSI, an agentic system for recursive self-improvement (RSI) in a real-to-sim-to-real setting, where task-specific simulations are constructed from target deployment scenarios and used as low-cost environments for iterative policy improvement before transfer back to the physical world. EmbodiRSI uses policy execution feedback to guide subsequent experience acquisition and policy updates. Across three tabletop environments and 14 subtasks, EmbodiRSI increases scene-balanced autonomous simulation success from 50.4% to 83.5% over two RSI updates
>
> **关键技术：** 递归自我改进 (RSI)

> Fine-grained visual perception enables vision-language models to distinguish subtle attributes and ground their answers in visual evidence.

### 🔥 #2｜GazeAct: A Human-Gaze-Annotated Manipulation Dataset for Attention-Regularized Policies

**Anupam Pani, Wang Yuan, Qifan Wang 等 (4 位作者)** |  `HKU`

📎 [2610.24507](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24507) | `cs.RO cs.AI`

🎯 **Robotics / Data & Pre-training** · 综合分 `9.52`

> Vision-language-action (VLA) policies must ground language instructions in the right objects, contact points, and target locations for reliable manipulation. Human gaze provides a natural signal for this grounding, but existing robot datasets rarely pair real human gaze with the multi-camera observations used by modern policies. We introduce GazeAct, a gaze-equipped real-robot manipulation dataset and training framework for multi-view VLA policies. We transfer gaze from the operator-selected view to the remaining synchronized cameras, producing spatially consistent targets across the policy's visual inputs. We then use these gaze-derived targets to regularize language-conditioned visual attention during training while keeping inference gaze-free
>

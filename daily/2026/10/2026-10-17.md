### 🔥 #2｜What Should Robot Policies Learn to Predict? Rethinking Supervision Targets in Vision-Language-Action Learning

**Haitao Shen, Hanyu Liu, Yihan Lin 等 (10 位作者)** |  `Tsinghua, PKU, BJTU`

📎 [2610.21915](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21915) | `cs.RO`

> Vision-language-action (VLA) policies are typically trained with action prediction as the primary supervision signal, since robots ultimately execute actions. Auxiliary objectives such as latent-action (LA) and future-vision (FV) prediction are often added to improve policy learning, yet they compete with action prediction for the same training budget. We ask whether, under a fixed budget, allocating a substantial share of training samples to such objectives, rather than to further action prediction, can improve policy learning. We build a unified VLA framework that couples a two-stream multimodal diffusion transformer (MMDiT) with a sample-level router, which assigns each training sample to either action prediction or an auxiliary objective and thus makes supervision allocation explicit
>
> **关键技术：** 扩散模型 / Transformer / VLA

### 🔥 #4｜Spark-0: Scaling Spatially Aligned Tactile Pretraining for Robot Dexterous Manipulation

**Tianxing Chen, Wenwei Lin, Chuqiao Lyu 等 (22 位作者)** |  `Tsinghua, PKU, HKUST, HKU`

📎 [2610.22342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22342) | `cs.RO`

> Generalist robot policies benefit from large-scale visual pretraining but often struggle with contact-rich dexterous manipulation, where force, compliance, and incipient slip are hard to observe from images. We present Spark-0, a spatially aligned vision-tactile-language-action (VTLA) model that uses touch in two complementary ways. During pretraining, image-space contact maps derived from tactile gloves, together with features from a frozen 3D geometry teacher, supervise the visual tokens; these objectives are removed at inference. ### 🔥 #5｜VeriVLA: Adaptive Test-Time Verification for Flow-Based VLAs with Latent World Models

**Xueli Sun, Yuxiang Lu, Yantong Liu 等 (7 位作者)** |  `HKU`

📎 [2610.21985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21985) | `cs.RO`

> Flow-based Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies are inherently stochastic: different noise seeds can produce action chunks of varying quality from the same observation. At test time, however, the policy typically commits to a single sample without an explicit estimate of its quality. In fine-grained manipulation, executing a suboptimal action can irreversibly disrupt task progress and lead to failure. To exploit policy stochasticity, we introduce a training-free two-axis trigger that uses two initial samples to measure within-chunk temporal variation and cross-sample variance, detecting the critical states. Once triggered, a JEPA-based latent world model ranks candidate action chunks by predicting action-conditioned future latent states and the corresponding changes in task progress
>
> **关键技术：** 世界模型 / VLA

### 🔥 #7｜Value as Memory: Online Value-Guided Post-training for General Robot Policies

**Wenye Yu, Yijin Chen, Kaiyi Xiong 等 (10 位作者)** |  `SJTU`

📎 [2610.22368](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22368) | `cs.RO`

### 🔥 #1｜EqC: Equilibrium Energy Control for Introspective Lifelong Robot Learning

**Haodong Yang, Runhan Huang, Bowen Zhang 等 (10 位作者)** |  `Princeton, Tsinghua`

📎 [2610.18790](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18790) | `cs.RO`

> Long-term robot autonomy depends on more than effective action generation: a policy must recognize when its behavior is no longer sufficiently supported and use assistance to improve. We introduce Equilibrium Energy Control (EqC), a framework for introspective lifelong robot learning built around an explicit conditional energy landscape. EqC casts generative action refinement as equilibrium-seeking optimization, while the geometry of the refinement process reveals whether the generated behavior remains consistent with the learned landscape.

### 🔥 #1｜ThinkWorld: World Imagination Reinforces Chain-of-Dynamics Reasoning

**Chaehyun Kim, Chi-Pin Huang, Jisoo Kim 等 (7 位作者)** |  `NVIDIA, KAIST`

📎 [2610.37666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37666) | `cs.RO cs.AI cs.CV`

🎯 **Robotics / World Models & Policy Co-learning** · 综合分 `9.22`

> Human videos provide much broader behavioral and environmental experience, while learning from them faces a fundamental trade-off between scalable data and explicit action grounding. We introduce ThinkWorld, a VLA learning framework that grounds action reasoning in world dynamics and strengthens it through reinforcement learning with world imagination. ThinkWorld represents each action thought as a Chain-of-Dynamics (CoD), a sequence of discrete motion tokens that captures the intermediate dynamics connecting the current world to a future state and serves as a common action representation across embodiments. This enables videos without action annotations to provide direct supervision from observed state transitions, allowing human and robot experience to refine the same reasoning space

> Open-vocabulary 3D occupancy prediction represents scene geometry and semantics in a unified voxel grid, enabling recognition beyond predefined categories. One approach reconstructs scenes with 3D Gaussians and converts them into voxel occupancy. 🎯 **Robotics / VLA Architecture** · 综合分 `5.13`

> Maintaining contact in non-prehensile manipulation requires more than nominal friction cone feasibility: a force barely inside the cone and one well inside it are both feasible, but they provide different margins against slip and contact loss.

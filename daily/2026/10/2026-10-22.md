### 🔥 #2｜Toward Recursive Self-Improvement in Physical AI

**Meng Luo, Zechen Bai, Shanqing Xu 等 (12 位作者)** |  `SJTU, HUST, HKU, NTU`

🎯 **Robotics / RL & Policy Optimization** · 综合分 `8.15`

> Recursive self-improvement (RSI) describes a system that improves not only its behavior but also the process by which it improves. Recent digital agents test this idea by rewriting their own code and checking each version with executable tests. Bringing these mechanisms into the physical world requires learning from partial observations through costly trials, while accounting for changing operating conditions and potentially irreversible consequences. We position RSI within robot learning and trace pathways from improving robot capabilities to improving the processes that generate them. We examine how physical experience can strengthen these improvement mechanisms and when the resulting gains can support further self-improvement of the mechanisms themselves
>

### 🔥 #8｜Divide-and-Remember: Recursive Action-Relevant Memory for Long-Horizon VLA Policies

**Xuehui Yu, Eason Yu, Meiyi Wang 等 (6 位作者)** |  `NTU, NUS`

📎 [2610.00982](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00982) | `cs.RO cs.AI`

> Vision-language-action (VLA) models struggle on history-dependent manipulation tasks, where the current observation alone does not determine the action, and the policy needs a memory of the history. Existing memory methods decide what to remember by design, for example, keeping frames with large pixel changes, and show inconsistent gains across tasks. We view what to remember as an optimisation problem. From the POMDP formulation of imitation learning, we show that the optimal memory maximises the conditional mutual information $I(a_t; m_t \mid o_t)$ between the action and the memory given the current observation. Intuitively, this means preserving the action-relevant information in the history that is not already contained in the current observation
>

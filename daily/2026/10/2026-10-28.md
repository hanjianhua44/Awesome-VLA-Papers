### 🔥 #1｜GAWM: A Compact Latent World-Action Model for Multi-Task Robot Manipulation

**Yang Liu, Xiaoli Liu, Liuzhao Li 等 (6 位作者)** |  `Tsinghua`

📎 [2610.37091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37091) | `cs.RO`

🎯 **Robotics / World Models & Policy Co-learning** · 综合分 `10.77`

> World-action models (WAMs) incorporate the prediction of future observations into robot policy learning. However, most WAMs build on billion-parameter vision-language or video-generation backbones, which incur high training cost and inference latency. In this letter, we present GAWM (Geometry-Aware World Model), a compact multi-task WAM that predicts future observations in the latent space of a frozen DINOv3 encoder. GAWM compresses the features of each camera view into a small set of tokens. A lightweight predictor, conditioned on the task rather than on actions, forecasts these tokens at two future horizons. The forecast is supervised with the dense patch features of future frames, which requires it to encode the spatial layout of the future scene
>
> **关键技术：** 世界模型 / VLA / 预训练 / 微调

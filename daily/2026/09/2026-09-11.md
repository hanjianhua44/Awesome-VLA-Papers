### 🔥 #2｜FARM: Reading Failure Signals from the Internal Predictive States of a Frozen Robotic World Model

**Haoran Pei, Mingrui Luo, Senbao Wang 等 (7 位作者)** |  `CASIA`

📎 [2609.11445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11445) | `cs.RO`

### 🔥 #1｜Continuous Actions from Discrete Minds: Latent-Aligned Planning for End-to-End Autonomous Driving

**Ruoyu Yao, Yusen Xie, Qingzhao Liu 等 (8 位作者)** |  `Huawei`

📎 [2609.04070](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04070) | `cs.CV cs.RO`

> Bridging the gap between the discrete reasoning of Vision-Language Models and the continuous, physics-constrained nature of autonomous driving remains a significant challenge. In this work, we introduce LaPla, a unified Vision-Language-Action (VLA) framework featuring latent-aligned planning to seamlessly ground semantic understanding in precise motion execution. We first design an action tokenizer based on a residual vector-quantized variational autoencoder (VQ-VAE), capturing vehicle kinematics and encoding trajectory features into a structured latent space.

### 🔥 #5｜Beyond Patch Removal: Persistent Adversarial Effects in Vision-Language-Action Policies

**Enhao Wu, Fusen Guo, Yuxin Cao 等 (6 位作者)** |  `NUS`

📎 [2609.19669](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19669) | `cs.CV cs.RO`

> Adversarial patches to Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies can cause both immediate action corruption and persistent state effects that remain after the patch is removed. Clean, random-patch, deviation-matched, and fixed-direction controls distinguish adversarial effects from occlusion, action-error magnitude, and directional persistence.

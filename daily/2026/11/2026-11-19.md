### 🔥 #3｜VSpark: Speculative Decoding for Autoregressive Vision-Language-Action Models

> Our aim is to accelerate autoregressive vision-language-action models (VLAs), which generate robot actions one token at a time. > Vision-language-action (VLA) policies incur control stalls when inference blocks the next action chunk.

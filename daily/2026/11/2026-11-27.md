> Robots should adapt to unfamiliar environments and acquire new skills through interaction, then execute those skills efficiently with practice. Inspired by human learning through reasoning and practice, we propose RoboEvo$^2$, a framework for recursive harness-policy co-evolution. In Policy-to-Harness (P2H) evolution, policy failures guide a coding agent to search complementary Code, Code+ICL, and Code+Policy routes, consolidating verified solutions into reusable skills, routing strategies, and experience memory.

> Wrist cameras provide an action-centric view that is directly relevant to robot manipulation. 📎 [2611.30389](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30389) | `cs.RO`

> Driving models need to choose which capabilities to use and when to reconsider their decisions. We introduce AgentMoT, a Mixture-of-Transformers with built-in agent reasoning and system scheduling. > Pretrained robot policies decide what to do from camera images, yet some of the physics that decides whether their plans succeed is not visible.

### 🔥 #2｜DeltaForge: A Post-hoc Mixture-of-Experts VLA for Multi-Domain Autonomous Driving

**Bingzhang Wang, Mo Poorsartep, Yasaman Esfandiari** |  `UW`

📎 [2611.11856](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11856) | `cs.LG cs.RO`

🎯 **Autonomous Driving / End-to-End VLA** · 综合分 `6.97`

> Fleets of driving vision-language-action (VLA) models are developed one domain at a time: each operator finetunes a shared base model on private data, producing specialized experts that are costly to consolidate into a single model. Weight merging loses accuracy as heterogeneous domains accumulate, and dense mixture-of-experts routing is prohibitively expensive to combine them. We present DeltaForge, to our knowledge the first post-hoc mixture-of-experts VLA for driving, which consolidates N independently finetuned per-domain models into a single multi-domain model from their weights and a small proxy set alone. When different operators finetune the same base model, their weight updates end up sharing a lot of structure

### 🔥 #20｜Library of Hands: Fix the Hands, Not the World Model

**Hasaan Ahmad, Qizhen Ying, Yuxuan Sun 等 (5 位作者)** |  `Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial`

📎 [2611.12678](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12678) | `cs.RO cs.AI cs.LG`

🎯 **Robotics / World Models & Policy Co-learning** · 综合分 `6.06`

> A world model predicts how a scene changes after an action, and a planner uses it to search for actions that reach a goal. We propose Library of Hands (LoH), a training-free method that keeps the world model frozen and corrects only the map from commands to applied actions, which we call the hand. LoH reads the hand through the frozen model, with no extra sensor: it finds the action that best explains the change between two camera images (a reading)
>

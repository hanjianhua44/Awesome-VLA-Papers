### 🔥 #3｜Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock? Temporal Shortcuts in Memory-Augmented VLA Policies

**Chubin Zhang, Bo An** |  `NTU`

📎 [2611.14488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488) | `cs.RO`

🎯 **Robotics / VLA Architecture** · 综合分 `7.83`

> Memory-augmented vision-language-action (VLA) policies are meant to remember what happened. Many of them, however, fill memory by time: they sample frames uniformly across the episode and stamp each frame with its time step. Because time passes even when nothing happens, such a memory may not tell waiting from working. We show that it does not. On the counting tasks of RoboMME, we make the robot briefly pause or fidget its wrist. Such a null event changes nothing the task depends on, yet a released memory VLA counts the interval as work done and stops early. We call this failure phantom progress. It cuts success from 47 to 16 of 50 on one task and from 44 to 10 on the other. When the same frames are withheld from memory, success returns, so the cause lies in memory rather than in the interruption itself
>
> **关键技术：** VLA

### 🔥 #1｜Concertina: Embodying Manipulation and Cognition in One Diffusion Vision-Language Model

**Chengcheng Wang, Wei He, Wenhao Li 等 (7 位作者)** |  `HKU, Univ of Sydney, CityU HK`

🎯 **Robotics / Action Tokenization** · 综合分 `11.24`

> Most vision-language-action (VLA) policies decouple manipulation from cognition by bolting an external action mechanism onto a vision-language model (VLM)--either quantizing actions into discrete tokens whose errors bear no stable correspondence to physical displacements, or delegating continuous trajectory generation to a separate action expert whose gradients compete with or are blocked from updating the VLM backbone. ### 🔥 #17｜VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding

**Yu-Cheng Chou, Shihao Wang, Jin Huang 等 (19 位作者)** |  `NVIDIA, Purdue, HKU, PolyU`

📎 [2611.08449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08449) | `cs.RO cs.CV`

🎯 **General / Efficient Inference** · 综合分 `8.35`

> Models for physical AI tasks, such as robotics and autonomous driving, need not reason solely in language. They can use visual plans--points, bounding boxes, and trajectories--to represent spatial goals and anticipate motion. However, autoregressive vision-language models generate these plans one coordinate token at a time, making richer predictions increasingly costly. We introduce VFlash, a method for fast, accurate visual planning through Flash Primitive Decoding.

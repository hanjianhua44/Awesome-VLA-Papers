### 🔥 #3｜What Can a VLA Learn from the Agent That Calls It? Task-Level Experience Distillation for Embodied Navigation

**Jinze Tao, Yuxiao Han, Kangkang Dong 等 (7 位作者)** |  `Tsinghua, Tsinghua Shenzhen`

📎 [2611.16246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16246) | `cs.RO cs.LG`

🎯 **Robotics / VLA Architecture** · 综合分 `8.64`

> Navigation agents use a large language model (LLM) planner to call a vision-language-action (VLA) model. Each episode needs the LLM, adding cost and delay. We distill successful agent experience into the VLA instead of distilling the planner. The VLA then navigates alone at test time. They include motions driven by planner tools and sub-instructions, along with detours. We present H2E, a task-level experience distillation method that converts these sessions into VLA training data. > Planning for a heterogeneous robot team from a scene image and an instruction involves two kinds of decisions.

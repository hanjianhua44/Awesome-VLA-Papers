### 🔥 #2｜How Much Should a VLA Trust Its Reasoning? Step-Level Guidance as a Measured Action-Value Function

**Dabin Seo, Eunhyek Joa** |  `SNU`

📎 [2610.40002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40002) | `cs.RO cs.CV cs.LG`

🎯 **Robotics / RL & Policy Optimization** · 综合分 `9.36`

> Reasoning vision-language-action (VLA) policies write a reasoning trace and then decode the action from it. A wrong trace yields a wrong action, and even a correct-looking trace can mislead the plan. Existing remedies ask whether the reasoning is right. We ask how strongly it should steer. CoC-CFG applies classifier-free guidance between the policy's context and the same context without the reasoning span. One weight per Euler step attenuates or amplifies the reasoning, and w=1 recovers the original policy. We treat the choice of this weight as a Markov decision process whose action values are measured, not bootstrapped. With the expert frozen and the noise fixed, applying a weight for one step and finishing the plan under the current controller returns that weight's value exactly
>
> **关键技术：** VLA

### 🔥 #1｜TinyAstral: Transferring Self-Evolving Harnesses to Tiny Generalist Manipulation Agents

**Kehong Gong, Xiaoxu Zheng, Guanli Hou 等 (11 位作者)** |  `Huawei`

📎 [2610.41216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41216) | `cs.RO cs.CV`

🎯 **Robotics / VLA Architecture** · 综合分 `7.72`

> Robot agents built on vision-language models depend on both the planner and its harness: the tools, feedback, memory, and recovery logic around it. Can improvements to a large teacher's harness be carried into a compact agent with lower inference compute cost? We present TinyAstral, a two-stage approach. We first freeze an open-weight teacher and improve its harness through recursive self-improvement (RSI): frontier LLM agents read the teacher's trajectories and propose harness edits, and each edit is accepted only if it passes a paired statistical test. We then freeze the final harness, collect teacher trajectories with reasoning and failure recovery, and distill a small student that replaces the teacher in the same harness
>
> **关键技术：** 递归自我改进 (RSI) / 自进化

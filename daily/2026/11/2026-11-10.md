> We show that generalist AI models can control manipulators more reliably than specialist policies such as vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world-action models (WAMs). ### ⭐ #4｜VLA Handover: Failure-Prediction-Based Switching from On-Robot to Server VLA Models

**Takayuki Nishio**

📎 [2611.08987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08987) | `cs.RO cs.LG cs.NI`

🎯 **Robotics / VLA Architecture** · 综合分 `6.64`

> Large vision-language-action (VLA) models generalize well but are difficult to run in real time on a robot, and small VLAs that run there fail more often. Remote inference over beyond-5G and 6G networks lets a robot use a large VLA on a server, but querying the server for every action chunk limits the number of robots per server. We propose VLA handover: the robot runs a small VLA (S-VLA) and switches to a large VLA (L-VLA) on a server only when the S-VLA is likely to fail. > A roadside sensor can detect vehicles hidden from the ego vehicle, but a pose error shifts every detection in the same message.

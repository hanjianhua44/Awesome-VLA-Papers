### 🔥 #4｜ReRead: Wrong Predictions, Useful Representations - Correcting Frozen Robot Policies by Re-reading Their Own Representations

📎 [2611.20465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20465) | `cs.RO cs.AI`

> When a pretrained robot policy fails on challenging tasks, common remedies include retraining the backbone, adding an external visual encoder, or introducing a memory module. We argue that wrong predictions do not imply wrong representations: a policy can retain the required information yet fail to read it out as the correct action.

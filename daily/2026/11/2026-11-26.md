### 🔥 #1｜NativeDrive: Native Multimodal Foundations for Autonomous Driving

**Zehua Fan, Qi Jiang, Wei Li 等 (10 位作者)** |  `Li Auto, Tsinghua, SJTU, ZJU`

📎 [2611.32216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32216) | `cs.CV`

🎯 **Autonomous Driving / End-to-End VLA** · 综合分 `9.04`

> Autonomous driving requires semantic understanding and fine-grained scene perception for precise action prediction. Vision-language-action models (VLAs) inherit semantic priors, but low-dimensional action labels provide limited structural supervision. Some studies extend VLAs with visual generation tasks or adopt video-based world-action models (WAMs), but both approaches introduce appearance-reconstruction overhead, which is less relevant to planning. Native multimodal models offer an alternative to unify understanding and generation within one backbone. On this basis, we propose NativeDrive, a native encoder-free multimodal driving framework adapting the unified backbone for policy learning and introduce a dense supervision task tailored to driving
>

### 🔥 #2｜GeoVLDrive: Geometry-Grounded Vision-Language Reasoning for Autonomous Driving

**JiaHui Huang, Songyan Zhang, Hanbing Li 等 (8 位作者)** |  `Xiaomi, NTU`

📎 [2611.32510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32510) | `cs.CV`

🎯 **Autonomous Driving / End-to-End VLA** · 综合分 `8.65`

> Vision-language-action (VLA) planners inherit semantic knowledge from pretrained vision-language models, but their reasoning is rarely grounded in 3D scene structure and its evolution over time. We introduce GeoVLDrive, a geometry-grounded VLA framework for 3D spatiotemporal reasoning and planning. Multi-layer features from a 3D foundation model augment the vision-language backbone through gated cross-attention. A structured latent chain of thought (CoT) organizes future scene geometry, key-agent motion, and meta-actions into ordered, separately supervised latent states. GeoVLDrive achieves 91.8 PDMS on NAVSIM v1 and 90.9 EPDMS on NAVSIM v2

### 🔥 #15｜Sparks of Weight-Space Programming for Robot Foundation Models

**Kyle Coelho, Eugen Solowjow** |  `MIT`

📎 [2611.31715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31715) | `cs.RO`

🎯 **Robotics / Data & Pre-training** · 综合分 `7.67`

> We apply weight-space programming (WSP), in which a language-model agent directly reads and writes a neural network's parameters, to a large, pre-trained vision-language-action policy. The idea is simple: many policy failures are not random but repeatedly land in the same recognizable stuck state, so if that state is visible in the model's internal activations, a small edit could trigger a corrective movement while leaving the rest of the model's parameters unchanged. > Bimanual manipulation requires each arm to perform distinct motions while coordinating with the other.

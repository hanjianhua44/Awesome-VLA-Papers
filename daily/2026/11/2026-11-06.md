### 🔥 #2｜Compounding Physical RSI: Turning Execution Failure into Inherited Capability

**Yixuan Yang, Qiang Chen, Run Shao 等 (34 位作者)**

📎 [2611.06654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06654) | `cs.RO`

🎯 **Robotics / RL & Policy Optimization** · 综合分 `6.62`

> Generalist robot policies are trained on static offline datasets, yet physical manipulation unfolds sequentially across stages. Because a weakness in a downstream stage remains unobservable until the policy passes earlier ones, each resolved bottleneck exposes the next hidden failure mode.
